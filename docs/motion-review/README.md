## Production checks

Lint, TypeScript and the optimized Next.js build passed. Chrome checks exercised both Site entry URLs, three project case studies, Story and return, the local FAQ and email link, keyboard tabs, metadata, and focus containment. Slow 1 px scrolling, interruption by keyboard, opening the menu during a preview and choosing a destination during its exit were covered. The production run recorded no page errors or failed local assets. Horizontal overflow was checked at eight widths from 320 to 1363 px. Server-rendered copy and the static portrait were also checked with JavaScript disabled and with browser observers unavailable.

## Access and limits

Reduced motion keeps the photo and factual numerals static, removes word and digit travel, and lets the statement controls change state immediately. The reference hides its break action in this mode. Tablet and phone input were checked with Chrome touch emulation. Physical touchscreens, Safari and Firefox were outside this comparison. [Reduced-motion frames](reduced-motion.webp)

The GitHub original is 300 × 300 px and tightly cropped around Tanvir’s head and shoulders. That limits the silhouette, particle coverage and detail. Final portrait matching remains pending the waist-up photo and a repeat of the hardware sequence.
